# Rouletter playtest

Thanks for playing! It takes about 20-40 minutes. Please play first and read the in-game guide only if you get stuck, because how it feels without help is exactly what we want to learn.

## Setup
- **Play here:** the GitHub Pages link. The title screen should show the latest version in the bottom-right corner.
- **On a phone:** play in landscape. On iPhone, Share > Add to Home Screen gives you full screen. If anything is cut off, set Options > Screen fit to 90%.
- **On desktop:** any browser, or the downloadable build.

## What to play
1. **Run 1:** New Run on the European wheel. Don't read anything first, and play until the run ends.
2. **Run 2:** any other wheel you have.
3. **Optional:** a Weekly challenge or a named challenge (the MODE button on the wheel screen).

## After each run
1. Press **Copy report** on the end screen and paste it into your message.
2. Answer these in a line or two each:
   - Was the first night clear? What confused you?
   - When did you first feel stuck, or that a loss wasn't your fault?
   - Did any table feel unfair? Which, and why?
   - Your biggest spin: did you understand why it paid what it did?
   - Which chip or rule felt best? Which felt useless?
   - Did the Cage feel like a real choice, or did you buy whatever you could afford?
   - Was the game speed right? Did you use the > button?
   - When (if ever) did you want to stop playing?
   - Would you start another run right now? Why or why not?

## For the developer: what to watch
- **Night 1.** The balance bot loses about a third of its failed runs on the very first table (see `balance_report.md`). Does that happen to people?
- **Onboarding pace.** New players get one new system a night (side jobs, then conditions and routes, then Cage events). Is that clear, or too slow?
- **The free chip at every table.** Do people read the three options, or click the first?
- **The Cage on phones.** Is it cluttered? Can they place chips and bets easily by touch, including the long-press menu?
- **House Rules.** Does anyone buy them? Is the Pit Boss's price in chips understood?
- **Enforcers.** Is the Timekeeper fair? Are Ilsa and Big Sal too easy?
- **Meta screens.** Do they find the Pawn Shop's GOALS tab and the Casebook? Does the free-chip / Feats / Markers loop make them want another run?
